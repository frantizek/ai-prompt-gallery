## The Prompt

Copy this and attach a reference photo:

```
A striking digital mixed-media portrait where the uploaded face as reference appears to be dissolving into a chaotic array of jet-black ink splatters and dripping paint. The subject is posed in a dramatic three-quarter profile, lit by a single harsh spotlight from above (Rembrandt lighting) that casts long, dramatic shadows. The skin is rendered with hyper-realistic texture, showing subtle stubble and imperfections, but the top of the head and shoulders explode into dynamic liquid simulations of ink in water. The background is a clean, stark white paper texture with visible pulp grain. The phrase "Frantizek" is written in aggressive, dry-brush calligraphy across the chest area, dripping with simulated wet ink. The composition utilizes the golden ratio, guiding the eye from the sharp, focused eye of the subject to the abstract dispersion. Post-processing includes a sharpening filter on the liquid droplets to catch specular highlights, creating a tactile wet look against the matte skin.
```

## Variations

- Change the calligraphy text to your name or brand
- Swap ink color (dark blue, deep red, gold)
- Adjust the dispersion area (full face, just the crown, lower jaw)
- Change the background texture (canvas, parchment, concrete)
