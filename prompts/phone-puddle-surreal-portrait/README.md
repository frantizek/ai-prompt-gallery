# Phone Puddle Surreal Portrait Generator

Surreal digital artworks of faces emerging from a smartphone merging with a rain puddle on wet asphalt — cinematic, dreamlike, and high contrast.

**Generator:** ChatGPT · **Status:** Active

---

## The Prompt

```
A surreal digital artwork showing a smartphone lying on a wet asphalt road with rain droplets. The screen of the phone appears to merge with a puddle, reflecting the realistic face of a woman with striking green eyes and soft lighting. Her face seems to emerge from the water as if she's inside the phone or the puddle itself. The surrounding environment is urban, with blurred reflections of lights and structures, and a vivid orange road marking cutting through the scene. The image has a cinematic, dreamlike mood with high contrast and deep colors.
```

- Change the reflected face (male, child, elderly)
