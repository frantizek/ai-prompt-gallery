# Paper Cut Portrait Prompt

```
A whimsical yet moody poster design using a layered paper-cut style. The uploaded face as reference is recreated using layers of textured cardstock, creating a topographical map of the facial features. The camera looks down into the layers, simulating a 3D shadow box effect. The lighting is warm, coming from inside the layers (backlit), casting deep, soft shadows that define the nose, eyes, and lips. The edges of the paper are slightly torn or fibrous. "Frantizek" is cut out of the top layer of paper, revealing a gold foil layer underneath. The color palette utilizes sepia, cream, and charcoal tones. This is a trompe-l'oeil effect where digital art mimics physical craft. The depth of field is shallow at f/2.8, blurring the deepest layers and the very top frame, focusing on the eyes of the paper subject. Dust textures and paper grain are essential for realism.
```
