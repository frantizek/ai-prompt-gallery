# Halloween Diner Portrait Generator

Cinematic Halloween portraits in a retro American diner with Ghostface, Pennywise, and a pink milkshake under pink and blue neon lights.

**Generator:** ChatGPT · **Status:** Active

---

## The Prompt

```
Cinematic Halloween photo in a retro American diner at night with pink and blue neon. Three characters: Ghostface eating a hamburger (left), the person from the reference photo in black clothing drinking a pink milkshake (center), Pennywise eating fries (right). Table with bat stickers and fake blood. Scary but fun atmosphere. All looking at camera.
```
