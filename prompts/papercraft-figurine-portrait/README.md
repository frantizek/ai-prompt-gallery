## The Prompt

Copy this and attach a reference photo of the person:

```
Ultra-realistic cinematic photo of a handmade origami / papercraft figurine of a person standing on a crafting desk. Strict identity lock — the face must match the attached reference photo exactly, preserving facial structure, skin tone, expression, and hairstyle (same haircut and hair texture). The figure has a stylized paper body made of folded geometric shapes, visible paper edges and layered cardstock textures.

Pose: standing confidently with both arms raised in a victorious gesture.

Outfit: modern casual clothing made entirely from colored paper (paper red jacket, paper shirt white, paper pants, paper sneakers) with realistic folds and shadows.

Scene: a creative workspace with cutting mat, precision knife, tweezers, scattered colored paper pieces, and crafting tools around. Two human hands visible — one holding the figurine and another applying a small drop of glue on the hair area.

Lighting: warm studio desk lighting, shallow depth of field, soft background blur.

Style: hyper-detailed, photorealistic miniature, handcrafted look, cinematic composition, 50mm lens, high detail, realistic shadows, tactile paper texture.

Negative prompt: plastic texture, CGI look, smooth surfaces, distorted face, incorrect identity, extra fingers, blurry face.
```
