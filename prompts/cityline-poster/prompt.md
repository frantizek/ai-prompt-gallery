# Prompt

```text
Create a striking minimalist line-art travel poster of [CITY NAME], presenting the city as a stylish everyday urban scene rather than a tourist postcard.

Show one instantly recognizable street, avenue, alley, tram corridor, pedestrian district, or neighborhood intersection from the city, viewed at street level. Fill the scene with locals, commuters, cyclists, café visitors, students, shoppers, and passersby moving naturally through the environment. Surround them with authentic storefronts, transit details, local-language signage, layered windows, street furniture, and architecture that reflect the real rhythm of the city. Let important landmarks appear subtly within the urban fabric instead of dominating the composition.

Add a large bold title at the top:
"[CITY NAME]"

Style:
minimal line-art illustration, monoline vector drawing, editorial travel poster design, Swiss modernist clarity, architectural precision, dense but highly organized city detail, premium urban branding aesthetic

Composition:
vertical poster layout, frontal or slightly angled street-level perspective, strong central focal rhythm, balanced negative space, visually rich but controlled detail density, elegant poster hierarchy

Color:
use only one main ink color and one background color, automatically chosen to match the city’s atmosphere, architecture, climate, and cultural identity; no rainbow palette, no chaotic neon, refined monochrome silkscreen poster feel

Mood:
stylish, local, calm but alive, design-forward, authentic, timeless

Output:
ultra-detailed, sharp, print-ready, collectible poster quality
```
