## The Prompt

```
Create a hyper-realistic cinematic double-exposure portrait of a young man in profile, dressed in a dark formal suit. His face is calm and introspective, eyes closed, lit by soft dramatic studio lighting. Blend inside the silhouette of his head a surreal landscape featuring stormy ocean waves, rugged mountains, and drifting mist, seamlessly merging with his hair. Use muted teal and grey tones, smooth gradients, and high-detail textures. Add atmospheric depth, soft fog, and a painterly, emotional mood. Ultra-detailed, 8K, moody, expressive, fine-art style.
```
