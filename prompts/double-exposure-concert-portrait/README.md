## The Prompt

```
A dramatic double-exposure artwork of a man standing on a stage with his arms open, surrounded by an excited crowd with raised hands. All black urban outfit, t-shirt and pants, black cap worn backwards, well-defined beard. Behind him, a giant profile silhouette of his face with the cap turned backward, wrapped in smoke and bright lights.

Cinematic style with warm tones (oranges, yellows) blended with dark green shadows. Concert lights, lens flares, smoke, epic post-apocalyptic atmosphere.
```

## Variations

- Change the crowd size (intimate club, arena, festival)

- Adjust the silhouette size and opacity
- Change the outfit and cap style
