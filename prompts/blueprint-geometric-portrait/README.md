# Blueprint Geometric Portrait Generator

Hyper-detailed half-face digital art posters combining architectural blueprints, geometric overlays, and abstract color blocks with crosshatch textures.

**Generator:** ChatGPT · **Status:** Active

---

## The Prompt

```
A hyper-detailed digital art poster of a mysterious man with a sharp, intense gaze and blue eyes, portrayed in a half-face composition. The artwork combines architectural blueprints and geometric overlays with abstract color blocks of gold, red, and blue. The man's expression is focused and enigmatic, his face partly rendered with fine crosshatch sketch lines blending into digital textures. The background integrates technical diagrams, schematic lines, and layered design elements, creating a futuristic and cerebral aesthetic.
```

## Variations

- Change the color blocks (neon green/purple, monochrome, warm tones)
- Swap the blueprint style (electrical, mechanical, anatomical)
- Adjust the crosshatch density and line style
- Change the expression and gaze direction
