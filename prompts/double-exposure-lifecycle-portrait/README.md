# Double Exposure Life Cycle Portrait Generator

Surreal double-exposure artworks merging two female faces (young and old) with seasonal trees, cosmic skies, and the cycle of life.

**Generator:** ChatGPT · **Status:** Active

---

## The Prompt

```
A surreal double-exposure artwork showing two female faces in a vertical collage — one young and serene, the other older and wise. Between them, trees shift through all four seasons. Above, the Earth and cosmic sky. Below, fields and sunflowers transition into a misty forest. Ethereal lighting, soft shadows, golden highlights, dreamlike cinematic feel. 8K hyper-realistic fantasy art, emotional and symbolic.
```

## Variations

- Change the number of faces (single face with seasons, three generations)
- Swap the seasonal elements (desert, ocean, mountain transitions)
- Adjust the cosmic elements (galaxies, nebulas, star trails)
- Change the art style (oil painting, watercolor, digital painting)
