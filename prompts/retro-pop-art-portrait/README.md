```
A retro-futuristic portrait with 1960s-1970s Mid-Century Modern and Pop Art influences. Graphite gray skin, large square glasses with mustard yellow lenses, shiny black lips, Mod-style black turtleneck.

Flat intense red background with old printing paper texture. Thick black outlines, limited palette, high contrast. Royal blue hair highlights, flat ungraduated shadows, halftone patterns. Confident direct gaze.
```
