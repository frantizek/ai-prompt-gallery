# Cinematic Night Poster

A city-name-only prompt pack for generating cinematic night travel posters with glowing streets, illuminated signage, reflections, and a stylish after-dark city atmosphere.

## Prompt Type
City-name-only master prompt

## Difficulty
Easy

## Variables
1 — `[CITY NAME]`

## Best Models
ChatGPT, DALL·E, Midjourney, Copilot, SDXL, Flux

## Output Style
Cinematic night travel poster / urban nightlife print / moody destination branding artwork

## What It Does

Cinematic Night Poster transforms any city into an atmospheric evening scene filled with light, movement, and urban texture. Instead of treating the city like a static postcard, it frames it as a living destination after dark, with reflective streets, cafés, transit glow, local nightlife, layered signage, and subtle landmark presence.

The result is designed to feel polished, dramatic, and emotionally immersive while remaining easy to reuse by changing only the city name.

## Why It Works

- gives image models a strong time-of-day and lighting direction
- combines city identity with cinematic mood
- creates visually rich scenes without requiring many variables
- produces outputs that feel modern, premium, and memorable
- works well across dense urban, waterfront, and historic destinations

## Best For

- nightlife-style travel posters
- city branding concepts
- cinematic destination artwork
- moody urban illustration
- collectible evening city prints

## How to Use

1. Open `prompt.md`
2. Copy the prompt
3. Replace `[CITY NAME]` with any city
4. Paste into your preferred image generator
5. Generate and iterate if needed

- `prompt.md` — main master prompt
