# Keyhole Portrait Generator

Noir-inspired portraits viewed through a giant keyhole silhouette with warm golden spotlight, deep shadows, and theatrical atmosphere.

**Generator:** ChatGPT · **Status:** Active

---

## The Prompt

```
A mysterious man sits in a dimly lit room, viewed through the silhouette of a giant keyhole. A warm golden spotlight from a modern dome-shaped floor lamp shines directly on him, creating a dramatic cone of light in the dark surroundings. The man is dressed in a stylish black pinstripe suit with an open white shirt, leaning back casually in a vintage armchair, exhaling a subtle cloud of smoke. The atmosphere is moody, noir-inspired, and theatrical — deep shadows, soft haze, and a minimalist set design. High-contrast lighting, warm brown tones, and a cinematic 50mm lens aesthetic. Add a sense of secrecy, suspense, and elegance.
```
