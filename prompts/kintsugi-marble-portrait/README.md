## The Prompt

Copy this and attach a reference photo:

```
A luxury editorial poster featuring the uploaded face as reference rendered as a pristine white marble bust that has been shattered and repaired with flowing molten gold (Kintsugi style). The photography style mimics museum archival shots: 100mm macro lens, softbox lighting from the top left to accentuate the texture of the stone and the gleam of the gold. The marble texture is incredibly detailed, with subsurface scattering simulating the translucency of stone. The cracks run dramatically across the face, one passing directly through an eye, filled with reflective, ray-traced gold leaf. The background is a deep, velvet black to make the white and gold pop. "Frantizek" is chiseled into the marble base of the bust in a classic Roman serif font, filled with the same liquid gold. Atmospheric dust motes dance in the light shafts. The final render requires 8K resolution to capture the microscopic imperfections in the marble grain and the specular reflections on the gold veins.
```
