```
A hyper-realistic studio portrait of the subject seated on a modern black director's chair. Mid shot, 3/4 frontal angle, 85mm lens for portrait compression, shallow depth of field with focus on face and upper body.

Scene: professional photography studio with dark neutral background, soft smoke or mist effect behind the subject, subtle spotlight creating a cinematic aura.

Pose: confident posture with one leg crossed and one hand resting on chin or temple, stylish and calm expression.

Lighting: dramatic studio setup with single key light from above-front, soft rim light from behind. Dark moody tones, desaturated color palette emphasizing blacks, grays, and skin tone highlights.

Style: hyper-realistic editorial and cinematic photography, f/1.8, ISO 100, shutter 1/160, 35mm film grain, Full HD 1920x1080, 16:9 aspect ratio.

Atmosphere: power, elegance, and confidence. Subtle smoke diffusion behind subject, soft shadows on the floor, slight vignette, realistic skin texture and clothing detail.
```
