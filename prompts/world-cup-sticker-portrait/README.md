```

Overlay a portrait of a legendary female footballer from Argentina. Messy high bun with bangs, wearing the Argentine national team kit — light blue and white stripes, black shoulder details, generic gold badges, and a national crest inspired by Argentina and AFA.

Photo style: passport-style, well-centered, appropriately sized over the background.

Top-right corner: 2026 World Cup trophy logo in white.
Right side: vertical text "ARGENTINA" with a small stylized light blue and white flag.
Bottom: rounded turquoise blue bars for custom text.

Style: sports photography poster, ultra-realistic, studio lighting, vibrant colors, crisp details, centered composition, premium collectible sticker, glossy laminated finish, high definition, 8K.

Negative prompt:
no square format, no CGI, no artificial, low quality, blurry text, deformed face, visible hands, multiple people, simple background, dull colors, blur, cartoon, anime, bad anatomy, distorted logo, illegible typography, duplicates.
```
