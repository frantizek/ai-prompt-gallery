# Day of the Dead Portrait Generator

Ultra-realistic cinematic vertical portraits set in a Mexican cemetery during Día de Muertos with candlelight, marigolds, and nostalgic atmosphere.

**Generator:** ChatGPT · **Status:** Active

---

## The Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic cinematic vertical portrait (Instagram format) 50mm f/1.8 4K. Identity preserved exactly — no alteration.

Subject in extreme close-up holding a lit candle, wearing a black hoodie with orange marigold embroidery. Setting: traditional Mexican cemetery during Día de Muertos under full moon. Gravestones with offerings, papel picado, copal smoke, marigold petals on the ground.

Cinematic warm tones (amber, gold, terracotta) with night shadows, light grain, shallow depth of field.
```

## Variations

- Change the celebration (Christmas, Easter, local festivals)
- Swap the hoodie style and embroidery colors
- Adjust the cemetery setting (rural, urban, ancient)
- Change the time of night (moonless, starry, stormy)

Works with: **DALL-E** · **Midjourney** · **ChatGPT** · **Stable Diffusion**
